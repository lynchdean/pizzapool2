How it works
  1. Create an event with a vendor - a pizza night, a team lunch, whatever it is.
  2. Share the link. Each item automatically splits into individually claimable portions.
  3. People claim their share by name and phone number - no sign-up required.
Built for group orders
  • Large orders split fairly into individual portions, so nobody overpays or gets left out.
  • Participants don't need an account - just a name and phone number to claim their share.
  • Organisers stay in control: lock an event once it's full, and see exactly what to order.
  • Social by design, encourages people to join orders with people and get to know them better.
