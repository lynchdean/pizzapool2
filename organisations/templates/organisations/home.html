Built for group orders
  • Skip the queues. Rather than having to wait in line, orders are consolidated meaning you can send it directly to the vendor ahead of time.
  • Social by design, encourages participants to join orders with new people and chat with them.
  • Large orders split evenly into individual portions, so nobody overpays or gets left out.
  • Participants don't need an account - just a name and phone number to claim their share.
  • Organisers stay in control: lock an event once it's full, and see exactly what to order.
